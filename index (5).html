Amelia FAQ 💙

Quick answers before you start.

What is Amelia?

Amelia is an AI-training platform that lets you complete eligible everyday tasks from home. All current tasks and instructions are available inside the app.

What kind of tasks can I do?

Everyday activities like folding laundry, cooking/food prep, washing dishes, picking up toys, cleaning, and lawn maintenance. All other tasks are available inside the app.

Do I need special equipment?

Yes - a compatible smartphone (iPhone 11+, Google Pixel 6+, or Samsung Galaxy S21+; no SE, E, or FE models) and a head strap / phone holder.

Can I just hold my phone or use Meta glasses / a chest strap?

No. Your phone needs to be positioned correctly for the task to be recorded accurately so you can be paid. You must purchase the appropriate head strap.

Can I use Amelia at work?

Amelia is consistently growing. When commercial tasks are available, a separate post will be created about them.

Can I repeat the same chores?

Yes, as long as the tasks are available. A task may be temporarily removed if it's been repeated too many times overall - your app will tell you, and updates will be posted here too.

Can I cook?

Yes, under food prep - but 50% of the time must be food prep before you cook. If you prep and cook in roughly even time, that's fine.

Why would a task get rejected?

Common reasons include camera angle or visibility problems. Make sure: your camera faces downward, your head strap is on securely, your hands are visible 90% of the time, you're following the task correctly, nobody else is in the video, the footage is clear, and you wipe the lens before you start.

When can I do tasks?

Tasks are generally available throughout the day, as long as the task is available in the app.

How much can I make?

Earnings vary based on how much you want to work - some people work 10 hours a week, others 30+. You're required to work a minimum of 10 hours per week.

Is this a W-2 job?

No, this is 1099 work. Please set aside 25% of your income for taxes, since taxes aren't taken out.

Where do I go if I need help?

Use Amelia's official support on Discord. In the Amelia app, go to Account → Discord Support.

How does the pay structure work?

The more hours you work during the month, the more you earn per hour: 1-54 hours = $10/hr, 55-99 hours = $11/hr, 100+ hours = $12/hr. The higher tier replaces the lower rate for that month - it does not stack on top. Example: if you work 20 hours, you're paid $10/hr. Once you reach 55 hours, all of your hours for the month (including the first 54) are recalculated at $11/hr; at 100+ hours, all hours are recalculated at $12/hr.

Does Amelia offer challenges or bonuses?

Yes! Amelia often offers extra challenges and bonus opportunities - current challenges, bonus opportunities, new incentives, and updated requirements. These may change month to month, so check the app regularly.

How do referrals work?

Referrals are a huge part of Amelia's earning potential. Earn 10% of your referral's income, up to $250 per person, without taking anything away from them. Make sure your referrals sign up using your personal Amelia referral link so they're properly attached to your account.

Pay tiers, bonus opportunities, referral terms, and app guidelines can change. Always check your Amelia app for the most current information.
